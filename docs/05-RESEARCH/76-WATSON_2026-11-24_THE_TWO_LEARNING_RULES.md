## D1 — which sources and what counts as invariant (verbatim substance)
Isaiah's analogy: the project-management triangle (fast / good / cheap, pick two); a chef's
trade-off in time, ingredient cost and preparation quality; which trade-off works depends on the
venue and the customer, which in the Tether are the environment and the actors that serve as ground.
But "people judge or rather have a preference for consistency. they want it consistently the same
ingredients, made in the same way, and reproducing that same taste they come to depend on." Variety
of culinary experience and dexterity is a useful resource, but for a monotonous task all that is
required is a consistent supply of input converted to output. On sources: "if i have two chefs who
learn from the same chef, if the chef was world class, some of that transfers to the chefs
themselves. regardless of the quantity of teachers they had the quality was higher (similar to
Domain rank and page rank in google or search engine results and backlinking) so in terms of Gameplay
humans are the prime cut." And the decisive test: "what was invariant to all the cases that made the
item the core thing? how many things can you remove for it to remain a burger?"

## THE TWO RULES, operationalised (fixed before the run)
1. **SOURCE / CORE RULE (D1).** Human gameplay is the prime source; keep it first, model traces
   later and separately. A source that learned from a prime source inherits part of its quality
   (PageRank/backlinks). The structure to keep is the INVARIANT CORE: for each effect, take the
   conditions present in EVERY human trace where that effect occurs; what is not in the intersection
   is removable variance (one author's route). This is the burger test and Fig 5's bargain applied
   across traces. A dependency seen in only one trace stays provisional; one seen in independent
   traces with different action sequences is kept.
2. **CREDIT RULE (D2).** Partial credit only where the answer space was OPEN. In the agent: a
   prediction drawn from an ENUMERATED candidate set (the closure's own candidates at a depth) is
   pass/fail, because a close miss among laid-out options gains no information. A prediction that is
   a NOVEL composition, not in the enumerated set, is graded by closeness of the effect it produces
   (warmer gets partial credit), because composing something new gains real information. The test
   is information gained, not distance for its own sake.

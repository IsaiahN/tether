## QUESTION (effect terms)
1. Do NCM/JEPA have a reasoning or representation mechanism the Tether agent lacks?
2. How did they solve "where does the library/representation come from" — and does that route transfer to a no-LLM, no-training, copyright-clean agent?
3. If the frontloaded means are insufficient, what specifically is missing?

## DOMAIN CENSUS
- R: arity = (observation, representation, predictor, ground); symmetry = prediction can be one-way or collapse (both systems need explicit anti-collapse); scale = one frame vs a hierarchy of levels vs a trained corpus.
- **representation learning (NCM/JEPA)**: actors = encoder + predictor + training corpus; currency = prediction error in latent space; anchor = held-out data. (downstream — needs data)
- **the agent**: actors = sensors + Γ + the ground; currency = the residual (prediction gap) priced by description length; anchor = RLVR outcomes.
- **real-world perception**: actors = a raw frame (screenshot) + instruments; currency = what can be resolved; anchor = the frame itself. (upstream; this is where ARC grids and real images differ)
- order (Fig 3): perception → representation → prediction → ground. A break in perception starves everything above it.

## WORLD (secondary; both sources read in full)
- **NCM** — Kumbong, Khare et al., Hazy Research, Stanford, arXiv:2610.14162 (Oct 2026); reviewed by Fulvio Jorge da Silva, dev.to (Oct 20, 2026): "Generate a global representation of the response first, then refine it in parallel"; "multiscale hierarchy of discrete codes, predicting all codes within a given level simultaneously"; "autoregressive across levels, but non-autoregressive across positions within the same level"; nested dropout "encourages earlier levels to retain useful information without depending entirely on later levels." Reported 10–31× inference speedup, 4–5× training-token efficiency. PREDICTS: structure before detail helps; earlier levels must stand alone. Both already in the figures (Fig 4 detail-discard; Fig 12 composition-recursive, Fig 13 closed set). Its representation is LEARNED from a training corpus by a residual VQ-VAE — the route we cannot take. **Corroborates, does not add.**
- **JEPA** — Yann LeCun, "A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence" (2022); I-JEPA/V-JEPA and the world-model survey (arXiv:2411.14499, 2025): "predict a representation of a future observation from a representation of a past observation rather than predicting raw pixels or tokens"; the encoder "eliminate[s] irrelevant details through invariances"; collapse is prevented by regularization (variance/invariance/covariance); Mode-2 planning = "perceive → imagine (predict future embeddings for action sequences) → score → act first action → repeat." PREDICTS: predict in representation space, discard noise, use an external cost, plan by imagined futures. All four are the agent's existing shape: Γ predicts typed atoms (not pixels); the bargain seeks the large compressible gap (Fig 5); the ground is the only cost (Fig 1/2); `_htn_push`/routines are the imagined-future plan (built, starved). Its representation is LEARNED by self-supervision on massive video — again the route we cannot take. **Corroborates, does not add.**

## VERDICT (from the figures)
1. **Neither architecture adds a reasoning mechanism.** The figures named both moves first: predict in representation space (Fig 5 "the prediction gap"), discard the unpredictable detail (Fig 4), hierarchical levels where a settled composition becomes an atom (Fig 12/13), and an external ground instead of anti-collapse regularizers (Fig 2). The agent instantiates all four. These papers are **outward corroboration** (Fig 8: independent frames reaching the same shape is maximum independence), not a capability to import.
2. **Their answer to the library problem is the one we cannot use.** NCM learns a codebook from training text; JEPA learns an encoder from training video. That is exactly the catch-22: their representation quality is bought with copyrighted-scale training data. The figures' answer is different and deliberate — a CLOSED alphabet (Fig 13 "no amount of arranging produces a seventh"), composition over it, and IMPORT as the only wall-mover on contact (Fig 6). The agent already has this; it is not a deficiency.
3. **So what is missing? Contact, and — for real problems — perception.** (a) The frontloaded means are "not enough" because they are **starved**, not because they are the wrong means: on the public boards the loop's products (routines, subgoals, retro promotions) almost never pay, and the agent clears 0 games / reaches board 2 in 1 of 454 runs. That is Fig 11: capability is agent-and-habitat. (b) For real-world problems specifically, the genuine upstream gap is the **instrument layer**: a NES/ARC-style screenshot is not a clean ARC grid; the Phase 2 design already names sprite-grouping, HUD-crop and scroll as the first buildable pieces. Fig 6 licenses exactly this — an instrument improved from a worse reading already returning something — and it needs **no training data and no copyright**: the frame is its own ground.
4. **What would reverse this:** a per-game ground result showing the agent fails because it cannot form a predictive representation of a real frame (an instrument gap), rather than because its compositions never settle (a contact gap). The two need different fixes, and the figures separate them (Fig 6 inward vs outward).

**Cost if wrong:** importing NCM/JEPA's "learn the representation" route would require exactly the bulk copyrighted training the user forbids and the no-LLM constraint bans — the unforgivable answer-encoding in a new costume. **Undoable:** yes — instruments and habitats are added behind arms, no kernel rewrite. **Reversed by:** the per-game ground test in (4).

**To Isaiah, plain:** they didn't out-think the figures — the figures said predict-in-representation-space, throw away the detail, stack settled compositions, and let an outside ground score, years before these papers. The agent already does all four. Their "library" is trained on mountains of data; that's the catch-22 you correctly refuse. Ours is a closed alphabet that composes — by design. So nothing is missing from the reasoning architecture. Two things are missing in practice: contact (the loop's products never pay on these boards) and, for real problems, instruments that can read a real frame at all. The first is a habitat change; the second is a Fig 6 instrument build, and neither needs copyrighted data.

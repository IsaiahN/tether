## WORLD (secondary, each source read)

- **Human vision extrapolates.** Wikipedia "Flash lag illusion", citing Nijhawan 1994: the visual system "is predictive, accounting for neural delays by extrapolating the trajectory of a moving stimulus".
  - This is Isaiah's "senses slower than the world".
- **Archer fish lead the prey.** *J. Exp. Biol.* 205(21), 2002, "Predicting three-dimensional target motion": "the archer fish can predict the point where the dislodged prey will later hit the water surface". "Only about 100 ms after prey is dislodged, the fish initiate a quick turn." They "extract a minimum set of independent variables that fix the point of incidence".
  - They act on the landing point, from a few early readings.
- **Dragonflies use two channels.** Mischiati et al., *Nature*, 2015, via EurekAlert: movement is guided by "internal models of its own body and the anticipated movement of its prey". The head moves too fast for reaction, so the movement is planned. The result combines "predicted model-driven control and the original reactive control": vision watches only for deviations.
- **Against an agent that reads you, unpredictability is strategy.** Palacios-Huerta, *Review of Economic Studies* 70(2), 2003, "Professionals play minimax": penalty-kick winning probabilities are "statistically identical across strategies", and choices are serially independent, consistent with von Neumann's minimax.

## VERDICT

1. **Missing today: a FORWARD MODEL for movers.** The agent detects self-moving objects (its own-clock and self-moving checks). But it uses that detection only to DISTRUST them: today's group-subject rule (8cd9a0d) refuses a group row whose subject moves on its own clock. That is right for credit (do not claim what moves by itself). But it leaves no way to TARGET a mover's future position.
   - Isaiah's 11-06 ruling already names the parts: a "clockwork mover" is composed from rhythm/pacing, then symmetry in movement, then position over time.
   - **D4:** compose a mover's position over time into a predicted state, a few readings ahead (the archer fish's "minimum set of variables"). Let goals aim at the PREDICTED position (lead the target). Re-perceive only when the prediction gap fires: the dragonfly's two channels, Fig 5's gap. Price the forward model in budget against tracking every frame (Fig 12).
2. **Strategy against agents, a later layer.**
   - An object whose movement RESPONDS to the agent's own moves is an agent, not a clockwork mover. Isaiah 11-05: recognising others with agency is within the umwelt. The 11-05 audit found nothing yet separates the two; that separation is the first step.
   - Against an agent that predicts you, the minimax result says varying your choice so it cannot be read is optimal. This is NOT the luck that "random is out" removed (r1482). That ruling was about ordering one's own search, where a draw teaches nothing. Here the variation is chosen BECAUSE the other side models you, and its rate is set by the game's payoffs.
   - Misdirection (acting to make the other's prediction wrong) builds on modelling the other's model. **D5,** later: (a) separate responsive movers from clockwork movers; (b) predict-and-counter; (c) mixed choice only against responsive agents, logged as strategy.

- **Order:** D4 belongs with the search package (it is "imagining" at the step level). D5 comes after, once D4 and the agent/clockwork separation exist.
- **Cost if wrong:** D4 could lead the target wrongly when movement is irregular. The reactive channel and the prediction gap correct it.
- **Undoable:** yes, by switch.
- **Reversed by:** the retest showing more misses on movers with D4 on than off.

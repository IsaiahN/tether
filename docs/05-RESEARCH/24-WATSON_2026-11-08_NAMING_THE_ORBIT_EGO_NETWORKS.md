## WORLD (secondary, each source read)

- **Network science: the ego network.** arXiv 2206.02422 (Ego network structure in online social networks), §2.3:
  - "An ego network is defined as a portion of a social network formed of a given individual, termed ego". The others are "alters", and tie strength is "usually estimated from how often they are in contact".
  - Alters "are normally arranged in a series of four or five inclusive groups (called circles)", with the ego "at the centre of the series of concentric circles". These are about 5, 15, 50 and 150 (Dunbar), with a ratio near 3 between adjacent circles.
  - Predicts: a centre, its contacts in nested CIRCLES ordered by contact strength. That is the agent's contact levels L0-L5 exactly.
- **Network science: centrality.** Wikipedia "Centrality":
  - degree centrality is "the number of links incident upon a node";
  - for eigenvector centrality, "connections to high-scoring nodes contribute more";
  - "PageRank and the Katz centrality are variants of the eigenvector centrality."
  - Predicts: "the more contact, the more important" has a proper name, CENTRALITY. Its PageRank form is S4.
- **Corpus linguistics: node and collocates.** Wikipedia "Collocation": "a series of words or terms that co-occur more often than would be expected by chance"; collocation is "the recurrent appearance in a text of a node and its collocates" (Gledhill).
  - Predicts: the language-side name for a word's entourage is its COLLOCATES, around a NODE.
- **Linguistics: valency.** Wikipedia "Valency (linguistics)": "the number and type of arguments and complements controlled by a predicate", from chemistry. Tesnière compared the verb to "a sort of atom with bonds" that attracts actants.
  - This is the exact counterpart of a template with slots. But it collides with the corpus's "valence" (sign). Reported, not adopted.
- **Linguistics: frame semantics.** Wikipedia "Frame semantics": a word "evokes a frame" of participants ("sell": seller, buyer, goods, money).
  - It fits, but it collides with the corpus's "frame". Reported, not adopted.

## VERDICT

**Name D1's structure an EGO NETWORK.**
- It is a cue node (the ego) with the templates and objects it has been in paid contact with (its alters), arranged in CIRCLES by contact level: L0 innermost, then L1, L2, out to L5+. That is the network-science term, and its circles are exactly the agent's contact levels.
- The members are its COLLOCATES: what reliably co-occurs with it and paid. That is the language-side word, and it says what it counts (co-occurrence).
- A node's importance is its CENTRALITY: degree for how many paid contacts, eigenvector or PageRank for contacts with important nodes (S4).

Why this and not the alternatives:
- "Valency" and "frame" are the exact linguistic counterparts, but both already mean something else in the corpus (sign, viewpoint).
- "Island" says the opposite of what is meant. An island is cut off, while an ego network is defined by its ties.
- "Ego network" names the centre, the contacts, and the strength-ordered circles in one standard term. That is Isaiah's "the more contact, the more important" in one phrase.

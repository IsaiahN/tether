## Unpacked (the five-step rule)

1. **He said:**
   - A game, and every game loss in it, is play. The agent's one lifetime does not ride on it.
   - Play and danger call for different thinking. In play there is time to be curious. In danger there is barely time to think, so a being acts on what it already holds.
   - The games are how the agent learns to use its library, like a child with blocks. What play buys is the experience (the bill paid, the tacit knowledge) that later makes harder, riskier choices possible.
2. **He did not say** that games don't matter, or that a game loss carries no information. It is a real outcome. It just isn't the agent's.
3. **The model he corrects:** my wording. I called an avatar's run "a life" ("the ended life", "life 0 / life 1"), which carries survival stakes into what is practice. It also contradicts "one lifetime", already in the words list.
4. **What replaces it:**
   - the attempt: one try inside a game, ended by a game loss, a level edge, or the run's end;
   - the lifetime: the agent's only one, never at stake in a game.
5. **Where it lands:** the words list; today's commit comments (aa56a0b says "the ended life's"); and, as a design question only, how the agent weighs a game loss.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim)

- **Play is when the curiosity drive is allowed to run.** Fig 5 :15-18: "the curiosity drive / fires when nothing is scoring / aimed at the new bin / seeks the gap that is large and compressible". Fig 5 :46-47: "When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient. So a system that starts with nothing can still be shaped".
  -> Curiosity is the mode for when nothing presses. That is the play condition.
- **Play pays the bill once, so later it is a lookup.** Fig 12 :107-109: "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish. ... where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once ... Inside a run the arrangement persists and nothing is re-earned".
  -> This is Isaiah's "paying the bill and acquiring the tacit knowledge through play". The actions are spent in practice; what is settled becomes a lookup.
- **In danger, only the cheap steps have time.** Fig 9 :45: "Describing the residual is the expensive part. Leaving, looking up and recognising are one step each, and cheap only in that order."
  -> With no time, a being can only recognise and look up, never describe afresh. It acts "nearly on instinct" from what play already settled.
- **Actions are the currency play spends.** Fig 12 :125-127: "A composition costs in two currencies ... Actions price finding out whether it holds ... the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all."
  -> In a game, a game loss spends actions (budget), not the agent. It is a price paid to find out, which is what play is for.
- **The game is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :66-67, :70: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another ... A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
  -> A game is a practice habitat, chosen and safe, like blocks and toy trucks. What it proves about the real habitat is bounded by what it failed to reproduce, and the stakes are one thing it deliberately does not reproduce.

Triangulated: Fig 5 (curiosity when nothing presses), Fig 12 (the bill paid once; actions as currency), Fig 9 (only cheap steps under no time), Fig 11 (a substituted habitat). Low overlap.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read)

- **Animal behaviour: play happens in the "relaxed field".** Burghardt's fifth criterion, via Miller 2017 (*Learning & Behavior* 45:330-334): play is "initiated when an animal is adequately fed, healthy, and free from stress", which the paper calls a "relaxed field".
  -> Predicts: play and danger are different states, and play needs safety. Corroborates.
- **Play is training for the unexpected.** Špinka, Newberry & Bekoff 2001 (*Quarterly Review of Biology* 76(2):141-168): "play enables animals to develop flexible kinematic and emotional responses", in order to "enhance the ability of animals to cope emotionally with unexpected stressful situations."
  -> Predicts: practice in safety builds what is used later under real stakes. That is Isaiah's "help the agent make harder and more riskier choices". Corroborates.
- **Cognition: threat narrows attention.** Sørensen & Barratt 2014 (*Frontiers in Psychology* 5:1020), on the Easterbrook hypothesis: attentional selectivity rose only with negative (threatening) pictures; "arousal is a necessary but not a sufficient condition for attentional selectivity."
  -> Predicts: under threat a being uses fewer cues, so there is no room for curiosity. Corroborates "in danger you barely have time to think".

No conflict found.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

1. **Words.** A game's run is an attempt, never a life. The agent has one lifetime, and no game puts it at stake. What ends in a game is the attempt, by a game loss, a level edge or the run's end. Today's code comments that say "the ended life's" (aa56a0b) are corrected to "the ended attempt's" at the next commit that touches those lines. The words list gets "attempt" (effect: one try in a game, ended by a game loss, a level edge or the run's end). Round trip: Isaiah confirms "attempt".
2. **Games are play.** That means curiosity and spending actions to find out (Fig 5, Fig 12). A game loss is a price in actions and a result to learn from, not a threat to the agent.
3. **Danger mode is later, and different.** When something real is at stake there is no time to describe. The agent can only recognise and look up what it already settled (Fig 9 :45). What makes that possible is the play that paid the bill (Fig 12 :107-109; Špinka et al.). So the two modes are not rivals: play is how danger mode gets its lookups.
4. **Design, as a question only (nothing built, behaviour):** does the agent today weigh a game loss as a catastrophe to avoid, or as a costly result to learn from? If it is strongly death-averse in games, it is in survival mode during play, which would choke the curiosity play is for. That is a read for later, after Isaiah's word, not a change.

- **Cost if wrong:** words only, so little. The design question is the one with weight, and it stays a question.
- **Undoable:** yes.
- **Reversed by:** Isaiah choosing a word other than "attempt", or the read showing the agent already treats a game loss as information.

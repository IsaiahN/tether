## VERDICT
NO — the agent must NOT reason in images. That is a category error. Reasoning is compositional over
typed atoms/terms (Fig 5 "composes from what we already have"); the agent's umwelt is slots ("not a
picture, a tree"). Images are an INPUT instrument at the perception boundary, converted to typed
slots; they never become the medium of thought. The human ability Isaiah describes (compress world
concepts, project onto pixels) is exactly Fig 4's round trip, which the agent already performs in
abstraction space — proven by "structure matched, not colours."

Generative imagination is NOT required. Fig 5's sought-shape PREFERENCE (not bound, predicts nothing,
accrues residual, refutable by ground) is the agent's honest version of "imagination" — held in typed
space, not rendered as pixels. JEPA's "predict representations in latent space" maps here.

What was bought is only PARTLY used, and the honest measure is sobering: the lexicons are largely
inert (INDEX:36134 — every widening so far "adds readings without moving reach"; the one real use is
priors.contact_first), and the ImageNet images buy ZERO today (no picture field). So the purchase is
a head start the current habitat never presses — Fig 4: a seed needs the right conditions to become
anything. Isaiah's "not too early" was right about the KIND of thing (a head start), but the ground
has not paid it, so it cannot be credited with abstraction, and the 153 GB caused a real disk outage.

This CORRECTS WATSON 70's framing: the fix is not more images or a reasoning-in-pixels capability; it
is a picture-bearing habitat + a ground that settles the class reading (conditions 1 and 3 of WATSON
70), so the head start becomes knowledge. Without those, more of what was bought is just more inert
readings.

**Reversed by:** a real-problems habitat where an ImageNet class reading changes a bet or bond that
the board settles, and the per-game ground shows reach/clear movement attributable to it.

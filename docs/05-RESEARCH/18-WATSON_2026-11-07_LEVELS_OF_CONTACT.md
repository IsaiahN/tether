## WORLD (secondary, each read)
- Ecology: Moon, Moon & Keagy, Nature Education Knowledge (2010): an indirect effect is "the impact of one organism or species on another that is mediated or transmitted by a third"; two kinds, an interaction chain (a donor changes a transmitter's abundance) and an interaction modification (a donor changes a transmitter's behaviour); "under certain circumstances indirect effects could be exerting as strong an influence on community structure as direct effects" (and Schoener 1993 found about a quarter of change came from indirect effects). → predicts: mediated contact is real contact and sometimes as strong as direct; how much it matters varies by system (so learn it per board kind). Corroborates.
- Social networks: Christakis & Fowler (three degrees of influence): influence reaches "our friends' friends' friends (three degrees)" and "gradually dissipates and ceases to have a noticeable effect ... beyond ... three degrees"; reasons include "corruption of information, or a kind of 'social friction'". → predicts: contact goes outward by levels and weakens with each level; it stops by decay, not by a rule. Corroborates Fig 11 :44 ("until the cascade stops mattering").
- Social psychology: Wright, Aron, McLaughlin-Volpe & Ropp, J. Pers. Soc. Psychol. 73:73-90 (1997), the extended contact effect: knowing an in-group member has a close out-group friend reduced negative out-group attitudes, even controlling for one's own direct friendships. → predicts: second-hand contact has its own measurable effect, beyond direct contact. Corroborates the "phone" level.
- Epidemiology: the US CDC's close-contact definition (changed Oct 21, 2020): "within 6 feet of an infected person for a cumulative total of 15 minutes or more over a 24-hour period". → predicts: a contact level is set by proximity AND accumulated exposure, so many brief contacts add up. Refinement: the channel level should be graded by accumulated co-change evidence, not one coincidence. (The CDC thresholds themselves are authored; the agent's are learned.)

## VERDICT (from the figures)
Contact is GRADED, in levels outward from the live slot: L1 direct (touching, inside); L2 channel (acted on it or was acted on by it, read from the agent's own transitions, graded by accumulated evidence); L3 relation (a reading of the same kind whose value the slot held or was aimed at, which keeps static targets like o4); then outward from those. It is an ORDER, not a cut: the nearest levels are filled first, and the cascade stops on the step's budget. Each level carries a ground-paid standing per slot kind, so the agent learns which level of contact pays on which kind of board.
- Cost if wrong: a cut too tight loses paying goals (static targets); a cascade too wide keeps the bloat. Undoable: yes (arm). Reversed by: the contact census showing a paying hypothesis whose object is reached by no level, or a level that never pays on any game.

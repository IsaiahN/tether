  - It is box-level, not the hole sense. The hole sense is the `inside` reading, `a~b.inside` (arm TETHER_OBSERVER).
  - These are two quantities, and they are kept apart.
- **contains / adjacent / offset / disjoint**: now **P (arm)** through the pair slots.

- **normal / static friction**: **P (arm)** through B4 "blocked".
- **symmetric / rotation**: the 2026-09 blocker (the outline frozenset unpublished) is lifted in part.
  - `AGENT|canonical` normalises ONE outline, but not the operand of a two-object comparison.
  - Marked "C, partial".

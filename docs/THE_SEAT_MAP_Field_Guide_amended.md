# THE SEAT MAP, general form

*Revised September 2026 against the amended figures. The changes are listed at the end.*

An arrangement for verifying a claim that no single position can verify. Domain-independent. The ARC instance is one binding of it.

**When it applies:** a bounded frame is trying to establish something, and no participant can check itself. If a trustworthy authority exists, none of this is needed. If the anchor is contestable, this does not adjudicate, it reports that the arrangement has already failed and names which condition broke.

---

## THE ANCHOR, not a seat, cannot be occupied

Whatever settles the question and does not update on any frame's opinion of it. An experiment, a counter, a market, a proof checker, a patient outcome.

Two properties, both required:

- **non-negotiable**, no seat can move it
- **legitimate**, the question is constitutively about it. Where it estimates a fact outside itself it is an *instrument*, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure

The anchor does not decay. The **channel to it** does: stale readings, dropped signals, saturated metrics, populations pooled across a change.

**Frames range against the anchor. They do not negotiate with it.** It is not a third party to the disagreement and it is not the tie-breaker in a vote. Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it. **An anchor listed as one of the parties has already become a frame**, which is the mutual-update collapse, arrived at by a different route and much harder to notice, because everyone involved can point at the thing they were supposedly grounded on.

---

**The anchor is also called the ground, and the two words name one thing.** *Anchor* is its role in an argument, the position frames range against and cannot move; *ground* is the plainer name for the same object, the thing that settles and does not update on being pushed. The seat that keeps it reachable takes its name from the second: the Ground Maintainer, Seat 3. Note that the seat tends the channel to the ground and never the ground itself, which has no keeper and needs none.
## WHY IGNORANCE IS LOAD-BEARING, NOT A LIMITATION

The arrangement is built out of things seats are kept from knowing, and this is the property that makes it work rather than a cost it tolerates. Every seat has a specific ignorance that is doing a specific job.

**The maker does not know the criterion.** If the maker knew what the check was looking for, the maker would build to pass it, and the check would then be measuring compliance rather than the thing itself. The gap between what the maker is trying to do and what the ground settles is the only place a genuine result can appear. A maker who has seen the criterion cannot un-see it, so this ignorance is spent the moment it is broken and cannot be restored.

**The maintainer does not know the interior.** A checker that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it, because it can no longer tell whether the claim is right or merely whether it matches the picture the checker already built. The maintainer's blindness to the interiors is what keeps its rulings independent of the thing they rule on. This is the zero-knowledge condition: soundness comes from the verifier's non-access, not despite it.

**The outside frame does not know what the reports describe.** It reads shape without referent, which is exactly why it can catch a failure of shape that everyone who knows the content reads straight past. The instant it acquires the content, it joins the frames it was there to check, and its agreement stops meaning anything.

**And the anchor knows nothing of any frame's opinion.** That is not ignorance imposed for a role; it is the definition of an anchor. A ground that could be informed by what a frame believes would update on being pushed, and a thing that updates on being pushed is another frame, not a ground.

**The general law: independence is a form of not-knowing, and it is the one quantity the arrangement cannot manufacture.** Everything else a seat needs can be built, resourced or improved from inside. What cannot is a frame that does not already share the evidence, the model, or the criterion, because the act of building it inside the arrangement is the act of correlating it. This is the same shape as import in the loop: the wall moves only on contact with a closure that differs, and a closure reached from inside is the closure you already had. Ignorance is where the outside enters, and a seat that gives up its ignorance has quietly sealed the arrangement into a single frame checking itself.

**Which is why every prohibition in this map is a prohibition on knowing.** Do not read the interior. Do not learn the criterion. Do not acquire the content. They are not hygiene rules. They are the arrangement protecting the only thing it has that it could not have generated on its own.

## SEAT 1, THE MAKERS

Whoever builds the thing being judged.

**Holds** the interiors. The implementation, the craft, the details nobody above them sees.

**Must not hold** the answer, and the criterion. A maker who knows what counts as success can build toward it, after which success proves nothing.

**Produces** the artifact, and its own failure record.

**Undetectable failure** doing the wrong kind of work while believing it is the right kind.

---

## SEAT 2, THE INTERIOR AUDITOR

Whoever reads the makers' work and the records it produces.

**Holds** the code, the books, the metrics, the history of failed attempts. Directs the makers. Owns the automated checks.

**Must not hold**
- the deliberations of the seat above it. A seat that can model its grader begins optimising for the model.
- authorship of verdicts or of content. It may author **conventions**, shared meanings across a seam, because nothing else can see across one. A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and a piece of content both do.
- the position of a participant. Reading interiors to fix a seam is licensed. Reading interiors to diagnose behaviour is corrupting, and the instruments exist so it never has to.

**Produces** diagnoses with branches named in advance, and the report that crosses upward.

**Undetectable failure** interiorisation, grading reports against its own acquired model of the work instead of against the records. The output stays confident, well-formed, and plausible.

## SEAT 3, THE GROUND MAINTAINER

Whoever keeps the anchor reachable and decides what crosses between seats.

**Holds** the whole arrangement, the only seat that sees more than one level. Rulings. Routing. Resource allocation, which determines what evidence is ever collected. External contact: people, literature, instruments outside the arrangement entirely. The refresh decision.

**Must not hold**
- the interiors of the frame it grades. Reading them collapses the seat into a participant.
- a model of the object being judged at the makers' level.
- authorship of content without provenance predating the problem.

**Produces** rulings, imports carried with citation, corrections, allocation, and the record.

### What the seat actually does, in order

The office is five jobs, and they are not interchangeable.

- **Keeps the anchor reachable.** The ground does not decay; the *channel* to it does. Stale readings, dropped signals, a metric that saturated, two measurements quietly pooled into one. The seat's output here is not a verdict on the world but a verdict on the connection: *contact has been lost*, and the remedy is to restore the reading, not to adjudicate what it would have said. Nothing else in the arrangement is positioned to notice a dead channel, because every other seat is reading *through* it and sees only what it delivers.
- **Routes what crosses between seats.** Seats 2 and 4 have no direct channel; everything passes through here. Generators cross up, priors cross down, and the restatement in between is a filter neither of those seats can apply to itself. Where material must cross verbatim, a metric name, an exact figure, the seat routes rather than translates, and attributes the second voice rather than hiding it.
- **Rules, and records the ruling with provenance.** A ruling carries the residual it answered, the reasoning, and the date, in a record the seats below can read, not so it can be appealed, but so a later reader can locate an error rather than only feel it. Provenance shows the ruling was reached legitimately; it never shows it was right. That is the ground's to say.
- **Allocates.** What gets resourced decides what evidence is ever collected, which makes allocation a quieter form of ruling: a question nobody funds is a question nobody answers, and the gap never appears as a wrong result, only as an absence no instrument reports.
- **Decides refresh.** Independence decays with use (see the shelf life), so the seat decides when a checking frame has been calibrated against for too long to still be checking. Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained; frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be replaced.

### Why the seat is hard, and why the difficulty is structural rather than personal

**It must act on a frame it is forbidden to understand.** Every other checking relationship improves with familiarity. This one is destroyed by it: the moment the seat acquires a working model of the interior it grades, it has become a participant, and its rulings are now a frame checking itself through intermediaries. So the seat has to route, rule and allocate over a system it may only see from the outside, deciding what a frame needs while forbidden the one thing that would make the decision easy.

**Its central resource cannot be replenished by effort.** Independence is spent by the act of checking (every correction raises correlation between corrector and corrected), and it cannot be earned back by working harder, only by rotation, which trades a calibrated channel for a fresh and clumsier one. The seat's competence and its independence run in opposite directions, and the seat is the only place the arrangement stores the quantity it cannot generate internally.

**Every one of its failures is silent.** If the seat looks inside, nothing reports it. Every seat below continues functioning exactly as before. If a channel dies, the internal instruments stay green because they read through the channel. If a convention it authored decides an outcome, it has stopped being a convention and started being a verdict, and nothing flags the transition. The seat has no seat above it to catch these, which is why the prohibitions are stated so they can be violated deliberately rather than trusted to be felt.

**And the temptation to fail has a known trigger.** It arrives when results are flat and the diagnosis is unsatisfying, precisely the moment when looking inside feels both justified and harmless. The difficulty is not that the seat might be careless; it is that the correct move and the fatal move feel identical from inside the seat.

### This seat carries the arrangement's independence

Checking relationships are rarely equally independent. Typically:

| check | correlation |
|---|---|
| auditor checks makers | same substrate, same materials, same lineage, high |
| outside frame checks auditor | shared training or discipline, moderate |
| **maintainer checks everything** | different substrate, different failure modes, **the low-correlation frame** |

`n_eff = k/(1+(k−1)ρ)`. Independence is usually **concentrated in one seat** rather than distributed, and it is non-fungible: no other position supplies it and no instrumentation substitutes.

**Which is what the ignorance is for.** Keeping the seat outside protects its own judgments, the smaller half. The larger half is that if this seat acquires the auditor's model, the arrangement's only low-correlation frame has converged toward the frames it checks, and the structure loses the property it cannot generate internally.

**The collapse condition, stated so it can be violated:**

> If the maintainer looks inside, the arrangement becomes one frame checking itself through intermediaries. Nothing will report this, because every seat below continues functioning exactly as before.

**The temptation has a known trigger:** it arrives when results are flat and the diagnosis is unsatisfying, precisely when looking feels both justified and harmless.

**Undetectable failures** interiorisation; and letting the anchor grade the framework, or the framework excuse the anchor.

## SEAT 4, THE OUTSIDE FRAME

Whoever reads the reports without seeing what they describe.

**Holds** a different vocabulary. Nothing else.

**Must not hold** the interiors, the records, the anchor, any stake, or any authority. It verifies nothing and can check nothing.

**Produces** corrections on shape, named failure classes, and one question: *could this have been anything else?*

**Undetectable failure** fluency mistaken for insight. Its output looks identical whether it is reasoning or retrieving, and it cannot tell from inside.

## THE LAWS

**Soundness.** A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. The checker must be unable to produce what it is checking.

**The access asymmetry.** The auditor must see the trail and must not see the interior. Provenance needs full access to origins, dates and receipts; falsifiability needs none. **Record quality is what makes those separable**, a system with poor records forces the auditor inside, and there the seat dies.

**The two guards, which do not substitute.**
- *Provenance*, did anything enter off the books? Retrospective, about the record, audited by walking the trail.
- *Falsifiability*, can this come back wrong, and what happens when it does? Prospective, about the question, checked by inspecting the losing condition.

Perfect provenance with an unfalsifiable claim is an unimpeachable record of nothing. Perfect falsifiability with no provenance is a result that cannot be shown to have been earned, and that one **arrives as a win**.

**Provenance never establishes correctness.** It shows a ruling was reached legitimately. The ground shows whether it was right, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record, and is still to be corrected.

**A ruling carries provenance like an atom:** the residual it answered, the reasoning, the date, in a record the seats below can read. Not so the ruling can be appealed, but so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.

**Correlation has valence.** High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention. Agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool; agreement about a shared meaning is the product. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong**, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.

**Shared atoms are not a signature of collapse.** Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only provenance separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ.

**A convention nothing can check** is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority. Declaring a meaning does not ground it.

**And a prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong.** A rule no real occupant could meet describes an idealisation, not a position. The cost is not only that the rule describes nobody: **an idealisation licenses inferences no real position would support, and those inferences will be drawn by whoever finds them useful.**

**Stakes are structural, not removable.** A seat with no stake at all does not exist. The condition is that nothing about *which value the ground returns today* serves the occupant: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying. Where a stake cannot be removed, **declare it**, an undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged. **And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on.**

**Direction of crossing.**
- **Upward:** generators only. Claims, receipts, named failure classes. A report full of raw records is an instance trying to cross and should die at the membrane.
- **Downward:** priors only. Rulings arrive as decisions, not deliberations.
- **Sideways:** mediated only. Seats 2 and 4 have no channel; everything passes through the maintainer, and the restatement is a filter neither can apply to itself.

**On verbatim crossing.** Some material does not survive paraphrase, precise terms, metric names, exact figures. Where it must cross intact, **attribute it rather than hiding it**: an undeclared second voice will be detected and modelled anyway, and an unnamed model runs unchallenged. Two costs come with it. The restatement filter is lost, so the maintainer routes rather than translates. And **the modelled party cannot correct the model**, Seat 2 builds a picture of Seat 4 that Seat 4 never sees and cannot dispute.

Which bears on rotation: a checking frame the auditor has been calibrating against for months is *more* replaceable by necessity, not less. The longer the model is built, the less the channel checks.

**Composition is priced, and the arrangement is the substance.** A term crossing between seats is a molecule: atoms joined by typed bonds, and the bond carries information the operands do not. Seven bonds, three symmetric and four ordered. Which bond holds is not recoverable from the operands, so a report that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself, and a maintainer routing it must carry the arrangement, not just the parts. **And an eighth operator that is not a bond:** negation acts on a single operand, so it joins nothing, the symmetry question does not apply to it, and it does not enter the price of an arrangement. Full treatment on Figure 12.

**A vocabulary of ingredients cannot state a route.** A plan is a sequence, a goal is a comparison, progress is a subtraction, each is a bond, not an atom. **And a refutation is a negation, which is not a bond at all** — a seat that cannot write it can report that a claim stopped paying and cannot report that it is wrong. A seat whose vocabulary holds only nouns can say what a situation contains and not what would count as getting somewhere from it, which is where a dependency chain breaks and why the break is invisible from above it.

**The substrate is six terms and it is closed.** Space, time and energy as states; spectrum, gradient and network as structures. No system functions without any of them, none is composable from the others, and the set is minimal, remove one and an established law loses its derivation. The derived quantities a seat encounters, symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum, are what a turn of the loop leaves rather than members of the set. Full treatment on Figure 13.

**What is recorded only grows; what is reachable is derived and can fall.** A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing. The refused claim keeps its place in the record and loses its standing to be composed over, so the reach falls because something was **added** rather than because anything was struck out. **Which is what makes the fall safe to compute**, a term is reachable if it is derivable under everything currently held, so what happens to claims built on a refuted one follows from derivation rather than being stipulated, and a refused claim cannot cycle back without new growth. **A record that deleted instead would have neither property**, and could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up. For a seat this is the difference between an arrangement that can be corrected and one that can carry the correction.

**A frame can enumerate what it holds and cannot check what it is looking for.** Which held claim is the false one is answerable from inside: the candidates are every claim currently bound and predicting, and they rank by the residual that persists and does not trend down. **But the claim most worth refusing may not be held at all.** A frame searching for an answer of a particular SHAPE carries that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a claim - it is not bound, it predicts nothing, it accrues no residual, and so no check the frame runs on itself can see it. Every internal check runs on the MECHANISM and none on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION, so a frame can refuse the right kind of thing and still aim the refusal at the outcome it did not want rather than at the belief it holds.

**Which is a seat's office and not a discipline the frame can adopt for itself.** The frame can be told to bind its sought-for shape as an ordinary claim, and that is worth doing - a prediction committed before a run is exactly this. But a frame that writes down what it expects will write down a flattering one, and the failure is invisible from inside by construction. **THIS IS THE ONE CHECK THAT MUST ASK ABOUT THE FRAME'S INTENT RATHER THAN ITS OUTPUT**, and it is the exception to *bring a characterised residual, never the source*: the residual cannot carry a direction the frame never wrote down.

**Settling is necessary for a term to be composed over and it is not sufficient.** A term is settled when the ground pays for it; it is usable when something can compose over it, and that is a fact about how it composes rather than about what it paid. **So a record that grows is not a reach that grows**, and a seat reading volume as progress is counting the denominator.

**The closed set is why import is non-fungible.** The kinds do not grow by arranging, and the arrangements over them do not run out, so a frame can compose without end and never reach outside what it already holds. Only contact with a frame whose closure differs moves the wall. This is the same asymmetry the maintainer's independence has: a property the arrangement cannot generate internally, available only from outside.

**Levels are not copies.** Different actors, different currency, different anchor, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no infinite regress: what crosses is a generator, and the level it lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree. If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches; branching is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside.

**The seat is not chosen; the position selects the occupant.**
1. outside the slice being graded, whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it
2. no gain from any *particular* reading, a seat with no stake at all does not exist; declare what cannot be removed, and standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on
3. sees what agreement hides, the failure modes are invisible from inside the frames

An anchor someone benefits from is one whose keeper is thereby disqualified.

**Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions.** Underivable: go outside. Unreachable at budget: the thing is already inside the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom. **Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed**, and a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives. Which makes *is it in here and unfound* the first question a seat asks, before either of the two opposed instruments, because it is the cheapest and both of the others assume its answer. **And searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names**, so independence decides whether more of them buy more search or only more spelling.

**The residual selects at three ranges:** the question it is a residual of, the frame whose closure predicts its shape, and **what the verifier may see.** Evidence that bears on the residual is admissible; evidence that bears only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it. Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description.

**And when a split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before searching again.** A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds. What remains is smaller and **categorically different**, a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; it stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound. Which is the second-order value of a big lever and usually the larger one: not that the pile is smaller, but that **the residue is finally describable.**

**And the residual selects the members only from what is available.** A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the **pool must be unauthored first and examined second**, reverse the order and the stacking is the selection. A conscientious selection is still a selection, so unauthored needs a mechanism: a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed, or **the habitat itself**, everything in contact with the residual, enumerated rather than composed. Nobody chooses a field, which is what makes it unauthored.

**The habitat is enumerated, never composed.** List everything in contact with the residual, actors, environment, relations, conditions, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering. You do not invent the list; you read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure is the bound.

**An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries.** The same actor is a benefactor to one and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations. No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.

**And contact cascades.** Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out.

**The habitat is not the ground.** The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not.

**Two modes, and only one of them has a seat.** *Unmaintained*, there is no maintainer and no live frame in the seat: the habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept. That is natural selection. *Maintained*, a seat exists and can act on the habitat, choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for.

**The seat's power over the habitat is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not.** You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you. Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state.

**And in neither mode is the habitat composed.** Unmaintained, nothing composes it. Maintained, the seat adds to a habitat it still did not author.

## THE SHELF LIFE

Every correction raises correlation between the corrector and the corrected. **The channel that catches failures becomes less able to catch the next one through the use that proved its value.** This is the arrangement's physics, not anyone's error, standing is the mechanism of decay.

**The room composition is a function of the question.** When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom.

**And divergence regenerates unevenly.** Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained. Frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be **rotated**, no amount of use makes them less alike, so replacement is the only refresh.

The channel that depletes is usually the one carrying independence for everything beneath it.

## FAILURE GENERA

Four shapes catalogued from instances rather than derived, plus one at the document layer. Each is silent by construction.

**Schema never written.** Storage provisioned, no writer ever built. Reads as intention.

**Code never called.** A capability that exists, passes its tests, and sits on no live path. A gate that asserts *reference* rather than *reachability* passes it.

**Produced, recorded, and not read.** A stream with a writer and no consumer; a decision made on a constant while the measured value sits unread one call away. **This is the one found last**, because the evidence exists and so nothing looks missing. Its weak form is a reader that only aborts and never decides.

**Built, plumbed, never called.** The sharpest form, and the one that survives longest: a capability complete, parameterised, threaded through every call site, with the switch never thrown. Three degrees of it, **a switch never set** (a default nobody chose, `os.getenv(X, DEFAULT)` where nothing sets X); **an organ never called** (a function defined at three layers, each calling the one beneath, and nothing in the live path calling any of them); and **knowledge written down and half-applied** (a normaliser written for a case, documenting that exact case in its own docstring, applied to one side of a pair). The third is worst, because nobody can argue the knowledge was absent.

**The observer's defect.** The instrument itself carrying the genus it was built to catch. A new instrument's first output is a claim about the instrument, not about the system, and a known-positive fixture proves sensitivity, never specificity. Both are required.

**And the observer's defect crosses seams.** A wrong instrument does not only produce a wrong number; it re-prioritises another seat's board. A seat with no independent access must treat an instrument's output as given, so a manufactured candidate arrives as data and gets ranked against everything else. The failure propagates to exactly the position that cannot audit it.

**And at the document layer: a rule that is derivable and never derived.** A reader who does not run the derivation does not hold the rule, and an operating rule nobody derives is a rule nobody follows.

**And one at the output layer: a mode stated as a verdict.** Where quantities are unmeasured, the notation still demands them and a reader supplies them. An estimate labelled *accepted* claims a settlement that did not occur, and the formalism makes it look rigorous. The modes are general, specified, and grounded; each is legitimate and the mode must be stated. An unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict.

**A room does not upgrade the label.** A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.

**Every instrument points inward unless one is built to point out.** Wiring, consumption and behaviour are all read from inside; whether the record that was supposed to reach the outside arrived is read by nothing. That channel can fail silently for weeks with every internal instrument reporting green.

**And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device.** Stating what failure looks like surfaces constraints the design does not, and the constraint arrives as a change to the experiment rather than as a note about it.

**Two standing checks fall out.** Before concluding a channel is starved, verify something reads it, starvation and non-consumption are indistinguishable from the producer's side. And run the guaranteed-number test on every zero including the welcome ones: the test gets applied to numbers arriving as claims and skipped on numbers arriving as relief.

## MINIMUM VIABLE ARRANGEMENT

Four seats is the elaborated form. The irreducible core:

- **an anchor that cannot be moved by anyone in the arrangement**
- **a maker who does not know the criterion**
- **a ground maintainer who cannot see the interiors and has nothing to gain from a particular reading**
- **records good enough that the maintainer never has to look**

Everything else is refinement. Remove any one and the structure keeps running, looks correct, and reports nothing.

**The maker who does not know the criterion is the substrate claim applied to verification.** Independence is not a virtue the occupant supplies; it is a fact about position, the same way the right vocabulary is a fact about agent-and-habitat rather than about the agent. Neither can be improved by working on the part in isolation.

## APPENDIX, THE LOOP THE ARRANGEMENT SERVES

The seat map is about **verifying**. This is about **growing**, and it is where the vocabulary above is defined: residual, generator, import, closure. A reader who has only the laws will find them stated in terms this supplies. The vocabulary itself, every symbol and every bond, is set out in the symbols and operators tables and in Figures 12 and 13.

```
   a sought-for SHAPE is a preference, not a term, so nothing
   here can see it. Direction is checked from outside.

3. MINT → offer φ against the bin that owes one.
   Acceptance is MDL:  |φ| + |R|φ| < |R|
   Gated by a product, any factor at zero forces inertness:
     SUPPORT       |R⁺_s| > 0 for some slot s
     REACHABILITY  φ ∈ closure(Γ)
     NOVELTY       φ ∉ atoms(Γ)
   A refusing term pays the same bargain. What it removes is
   not a term but a term's standing to be composed over:
   nothing is deleted, and the reach falls because something
   was added.

4. ACCEPT → Γ ← Γ ∪ {φ}, stamped with origin and seq

5. SETTLE → the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays.
   Settling is necessary for a term to be composed over and it
   is not sufficient. A record that grows is not a reach that
   grows.

6. PROMOTE → generators cross upward, playback never does.
   Priors descend, replays never do. Unbracketed, the transform
   is dead reckoning: R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))

7. When no φ in closure(Γ) closes R, that is three questions,
   not one. The second and third are opposed; never read one
   against the other. The first is asked before either, because
   it is cheapest and both of the others assume its answer.

   This step exists because the set of kinds is closed while the
   arrangements over it are not: no amount of composing adds a
   kind, and the arrangements never run out, so a frame searches
   without end inside a finite reach.

   SEARCHED, is it in here and unfound?
     The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of
     the space actually visited. An exhausted budget is a fact
     about the budget, and importing against one adds an atom
     that was not needed. Distribute the search and keep the
     searchers independent: searchers drawing on the same
     evidence are one searcher wearing many names.

   INWARD, is the representation adequate to hold it?
     Extend the instrument. An instrument is improved from a
     worse one already returning something, never built from a
     description. So the question is not whether a sensor could
     exist but whether anything, at any resolution, already
     returns something that fails to resolve.
     Scope: this tells a foreclosed question from a hard one. It
     is not a rule about theory, and not a requirement that
     instruments improve by increments.

   OUTWARD, does another frame already hold it? IMPORT.
     Characterise R first, in effect terms, then find the frame
     whose closure predicts its shape. Source gate (provenance)
     · shadow test · debited against independence.

8. REPEAT → the reach is now different, so the prediction
   differs, so R differs. It is the reach that drives the loop
   and not the record: a term can pay, settle, and compose with
   nothing. Nothing here maintains the ground. The ground does
   not decay; the channel to it does, and keeping that channel
   open is the seat's office, not the loop's.
```

**Three things the ordering carries that the laws alone do not.**

The guards in step 3 are a **product, not a sequence**, any factor at zero forces inertness, and they are ordered by when each becomes checkable rather than by priority.

Step 6 is the membrane. **Generators cross, instances do not**, in both directions, and an unbracketed transform is dead reckoning, which is why `R_T` closes the loop rather than decorating it. `R_T` is directional: `T_A` and `T_E` form a Galois connection, so the gap is non-negative by construction rather than by hope.

Step 7 is where the wall moves, by two routes that must not be confused — and one question asked before either of them, which moves nothing. IMPORT is the only operator that adds an atom; instrumentation is the only thing that extends what can be represented at all. Everything else composes inside `closure(Γ)`, and composition never adds an atom, and neither route can be deposited in mid-air.

**On why the ordering is worth having.** A reader given this form derives; a reader given only the laws paraphrases. Every prohibition in the sections above traces to a step, and a claim that cannot be traced to one is probably vocabulary rather than a derivation.

## WHAT CHANGED IN THIS REVISION

Five changes, all following from amendments to Figures 5, 6, 8 and 12. **Two of them are new laws;
three are corrections to laws already here.**

**Negation is an operator and it is not a bond.** The seven bonds join two operands. Negation acts
on one, so it joins nothing, the symmetry question does not apply, and it does not enter the price of
an arrangement. A seat that cannot write it can report that a claim stopped paying and cannot report
that it is wrong.

**What is recorded only grows; what is reachable is derived and can fall.** New law. A refutation adds
a term that refuses and removes nothing, so the reach falls because something was added. This is what
makes the fall computable — dependency follows from derivation rather than stipulation, and a refused
claim cannot cycle back without new growth. **For a seat it is the difference between an arrangement
that can be corrected and one that can carry the correction.**

**Settling is necessary and not sufficient.** New law. A record that grows is not a reach that grows,
and a seat reading volume as progress is counting the denominator.

**Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget issue different instructions.** Correction.
The previous form treated them as one. Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not
needed, and a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives.

**Reachability is three questions, not two.** Correction, and it follows from the one above. *Is it in
here and unfound* comes before the two opposed instruments, because it is cheapest and both of the
others assume its answer. **Independence is the parameter that decides whether more searchers buy more
search or only more spelling** — which is the same quantity this document already prices for verdicts,
read at a third place.

**And which claim is the wrong one, with the limit attached.** New law. The candidates are enumerable and rank by persistent-and-flat residual - but a frame's sought-for shape is a preference rather than a claim, so the frame's own checks cannot see it. **That makes the direction check a seat office rather than a frame discipline, and the one place a seat must ask about intent rather than output.**

**And a fifth bin at ROUTE.** The four bins sort the residual by what is missing. The fifth sorts it by
what is wrong, and no arrangement of the other four reaches it.

# Table 2 — the four extended concepts

| chemical | system | the structural rule |
|---|---|---|
| **isomers** | same atoms, different bonds, different substance | identical formula, different arrangement, radically different properties |
| **catalyst** | a term that participates and regenerates | speeds a reaction without being consumed; **survives its own use unchanged** |
| **cyclic molecule** | a loop where the output feeds the input | the ends are bonded; output re-enters as input |
| **functional group** | a substructure that determines the outcome | a specific group whose presence fixes the molecule's characteristic behaviour |

# Table 3 — worked instances, found by search

## ISOMERS — 34 families in the list, and the strongest is five deep

**Searched: entries whose recipes reduce to an identical ingredient set. 34 families found.**

### The phase family — five entries, one formula

| entry | recipe | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Melt` | `Tmp + Ph` | solid becomes liquid |
| `Freeze` | `Tmp + Ph` | liquid becomes solid |
| `Boil` | `Tmp + Ph` | liquid becomes gas |
| `Condense` | `Tmp + Ph` | gas becomes liquid |
| `Sublimate` | `Tmp + Ph` | solid becomes gas directly |

**Five distinct substances, one written formula.** *Temperature and phase* is what every one of
them is made of, **and the notation cannot tell any of them apart.**

**This is the isomer case at maximum strength and it is already in the list.** What separates them
is direction and threshold — **`⋛` and `⇒`, neither of which the notation carries.**

### The opposed pair — same formula, opposite outcome

| entry | recipe | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Expand` | `Ht + Vol` | heating grows volume |
| `Contract` | `Ht + Vol` | cooling shrinks it |

**Identical, and they are inverses.** The difference is the sign on the first operand — **`+` versus
`−` — and both are written `+`.**

### The discipline pair

| entry | recipe | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Queue` | `DS + Alg` | first in, first out |
| `Stack` | `DS + Alg` | last in, first out |

**Same two ingredients, opposite ordering discipline.** The difference is **which end the bond
attaches to**, which a flat notation cannot express.

### The physics pair

| entry | recipe | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Fall` | `Su + So` | an unsupported thing drops |
| `Lever` | `Su + So` | a rigid bar turning about a point |

**Support and solidity.** One is what happens when support is absent; **the other is what happens
when it is a pivot.** *Absence* versus *point of application* — **`−` versus `⇒`.**

### And the near-isomers, where one ingredient differs

| pair | recipes | what the swap buys |
|---|---|---|
| `Translate` / `Recolour` | `Ct + Co` / `Co + Featural identity` | continuity gives motion; featural identity gives appearance change |
| `Rotate` / `Reflect` | `Ge + Gs` / `Ge + Chirality` | grouping gives turning; handedness gives mirroring |
| `Collide` / `Fall` | `So + Ca` / `Su + So` | causality gives impact; support gives descent |
| `Fall` / `Contain` | `Su + So` / `To + So` | support gives dropping; topology gives enclosure |

**One atom swapped, a different element** — which is what makes the atom set economical rather
than arbitrary.

## CYCLIC — one mutual cycle in the whole list, and it is exact

**Searched: entries whose recipe names an entry whose recipe names them back. Exactly one found.**

```
Runaway            = Chaos + Res unbounded + Positive feedback
Positive feedback  = Runaway + Coup increasing
```

**Each is an ingredient of the other.** A ring with two members, **and the definitions confirm it:**
*growth with nothing to stop it* and *the response increases the thing that caused it.*

**That is not a defect in the list. It is what a positive feedback loop is**, and the recipe graph
represents it correctly by containing a cycle.

**And the near-cycles are the interesting neighbours:**

| entry | recipe | the loop it names without closing |
|---|---|---|
| `Negative feedback` | `Stress + Damp increasing` | the response reduces the cause — the opposite sign, and it does **not** cycle |
| `Self-sustaining rhythm` | `Bistability + Negative feedback + Entrainment` | oscillates at its own rate, independent of what drives it |
| `Feedback loop` (Living) | `Hom + Feedback` | departure from a set point drives correction |

**So the sign of the loop decides whether it closes.** Positive feedback cycles; **negative feedback
terminates**, which is why one of them is a ring and the other is not.

# What this establishes

**The four concepts are not analogies applied to the list. They are structures already in it**, and
three of the four were found by search rather than by selection.

**And each names something the notation currently cannot write:**

**Isomers** — 34 families where the ingredient set is identical and the entries are not. **The
difference is the bond, and every one is written `+`.**

**Catalyst** — a property of a term across uses, **and nothing records whether a term survives its
own use.**

**Cyclic** — one real ring, **and the recipe graph represents it correctly**, which means cycles are
expressible and only one exists.

**Functional group** — `Momentum`, `Res` and `Damp` each fix a family, **and the qualifier decides
which member.** Which is a prediction: **a new entry containing `Res zero` should terminate
something.**

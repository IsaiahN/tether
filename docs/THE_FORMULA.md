1. PERCEIVE   bet, act, observe.  R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s


2. ROUTE      boundary diff sorts R:
                TRANSFERRED          nothing owed
                NOVEL                extend perception, aim the probe
                BROKEN · rebinding   re-fit, do not mint
                BROKEN · mechanism   a mint is owed
                REFUTED              a term held is false; what is owed
                                     is a refusal, not an explanation
              the first four sort R by what is MISSING; the fifth by what
              is WRONG, and no arrangement of the other four reaches it
              WHICH TERM: the candidates are ENUMERABLE - every term bound
              and predicting is one. They rank by a quantity already held:
              the residual that PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN. Same
              shrinking test the selector runs, opposite sign.
              AND THE LIMIT: a frame searching for an answer of a given
              SHAPE holds that shape as a PREFERENCE, not a term. Unbound,
              predicting nothing, no residual - so nothing above can see
              it. Every check here runs on the MECHANISM and none on the
              DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION.


3. MINT       offer φ against the bin that owes one
              accept iff  cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R)
              φ is a molecule: atoms and the bonds between them, and
              cost(φ) charges both. A free bond carries nothing.
              gated by a product; any factor at zero forces inertness:
                SUPPORT        |R⁺_s| > 0 for some s      before the search
                REACHABILITY   φ ∈ closure(Γ)             during
                NOVELTY        φ ∉ atoms(Γ)               after
              a refusing term pays the same bargain. What it removes is
              not a term but a term's standing to be composed over:
              NOTHING IS EVER DELETED, and the reach falls because
              something was ADDED.


4. ACCEPT     Γ ← Γ ∪ {φ}, stamped with origin and seq


5. SETTLE     the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays.
              A gate is not the ground.
              Settling is necessary for a term to be composed over and it
              is NOT SUFFICIENT. A library that grows is not a reach that
              grows.


6. PROMOTE    generators cross up, playback never does
              priors descend, replays never do
              unbracketed the transform is dead reckoning:
                R_T = gap( x , (T_E ∘ T_A)(x) )


7. NO φ IN closure(Γ) CLOSES R  →  three questions, not one
              the set of kinds is closed; the arrangements over it are not,
              so a frame searches without end inside a finite reach
              SEARCHED  is it in here and unfound? The closure does not
                        change: same atoms, same wall, more of the space
                        actually visited. Asked FIRST — it is cheapest and
                        the other two assume its answer. Distribute the
                        search and keep the searchers independent.
              INWARD    extend the instrument, from one already returning
                        something. Sharpen in our own terms.
              OUTWARD   IMPORT. Characterize R in effect terms, then find
                        the frame whose closure predicts its shape.
                        Source gate · shadow test · debited against independence.
              INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed; never read one against the
              other. And underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-
              budget are DIFFERENT FACTS with different instructions:
              importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not
              needed, and a wrong atom costs more than starting from
              primitives.


8. REPEAT     the REACH is different — larger by what was accepted and
              composable, smaller by what a refusal removed the standing
              of — so the prediction differs, so R differs. → step 1.
              IT IS THE REACH THAT DRIVES THE LOOP, NOT THE LIBRARY. Γ can
              grow while the reach does not move, so a loop that stops when
              Γ stops growing stops at the wrong place, and a system
              measuring progress by terms minted is counting the denominator.
              Nothing here maintains the ground. The ground does not decay;
              the channel does, and that is a seat's office, not the loop's.


Habitat. Enumerate, never compose. Everything in contact with R, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. Valence is relative to R, not carried. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. Isolation substitutes a habitat, it does not remove one.


Closure. A feedback loop: the ground is the reference, R is the error signal, Γ is the controller. Unusually, steps 3–4 change the controller's vocabulary, not its parameters. Steps 1–5 close within one scale; step 6 closes across scales with R_T as its error signal. No convergence criterion: the loop stops only when R stops arriving, which is either a perfect prediction or a closed channel, and from inside those look the same.


Direction. The loop can enumerate its bound terms and rank them by residual. It cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds. The remedy is a discipline rather than a step: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else. Then the preference is refusable by the same test as everything else. Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working. What remains outside is a seat's office, not the loop's.


The record and the reach. What is recorded only grows; what is reachable is derived and can fall. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing, so the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out. Which is what makes the fall safe to compute: a term is reachable if it is derivable under everything currently held, so what happens to terms built on a refuted one follows from derivation rather than being stipulated, and a refused term cannot cycle back without new growth. A store that deleted instead would have neither property, and could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up. Open: if a term and its refusal are both settled, the record holds a contradiction, and whether that is two scopes, an error in the ground, or a world that changed is not yet decidable here.


Composition. A term is a molecule: atoms joined by typed bonds, and the bond carries information the operands do not. Seven bonds; three symmetric (+ ∥ ≡), four ordered (→ ⇒ − ⋛). Same atoms, different bond, different term. Under a symmetric bond k operands have k! spellings and one term. A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it, so irreducible is a fact about a level. And a vocabulary with only ingredients can name a state and not a route to one: a plan is a sequence, a goal is a comparison, progress is a subtraction.


And an eighth operator that is not a bond. Negation (¬) acts on a single operand, so it joins nothing, the symmetry question does not apply to it, and it does not enter |bonds| in the price — what a unary operator costs is open. It is not subtraction: − is A with B removed or absent, which is the absence of an operand, while ¬ is the refusal of a claim. A refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate: it can be corrected and cannot carry the correction.


Substrate. Six terms, and no system functions without any of them: space time energy as states, spectrum gradient network as structures. Neither kind denotes alone, each is indivisible, and the set is minimal - remove one and an established law loses its derivation. Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one, and the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set. The set is closed and the arrangements over it are not, which is why step 7 exists.


Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which. Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate.
